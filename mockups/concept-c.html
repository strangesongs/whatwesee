Both Together: Rule + Fraunces

Combines A and B. The 2px border becomes a shared structural weight across both sites (vertical on strangesongs, horizontal on whatwesee), and Fraunces is the shared nameplate font for both site names. Two small changes to strangesongs; one small change to whatwesee's header. Everything else — palette, layout, body type — remains completely site-specific. The result feels like a family of publications from the same author.
