2px Rule as Structural Signature

Both sites already divide space with a hard line — strangesongs uses a 2px vertical border on the sidebar, whatwesee uses a 1px horizontal border under the header. Making both 2px solid turns this into a deliberate shared gesture. Same weight; different axis; different color. The typographic anchor: both wordmarks are in an old-style serif (Times NR on strangesongs, Fraunces on whatwesee).
